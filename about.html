About the Studio

Scented by nature. Colored by the earth.

Unearthed Artisan Co. began with a simple, stubborn belief: what you put on your skin should be as honest as what you put in your body. No synthetic dye pretending to be color. No fragrance blend hiding behind a chemical list. Just plant oils, grass-fed tallow, and clay, root, and mineral pigments pulled straight from the earth — the kind of ingredients your skin has always known what to do with.

Every bar of soap is mixed, poured, and cured for six weeks before it's ready. Every jar of lotion is blended slowly from grass-fed tallow and organic oils — the same lipids your skin already makes on its own, not something manufactured to imitate them. It takes longer this way. It's also the only way we'd do it.

Handcrafted in the USA. Nothing to hide.

In memory of my father, who taught me to trust what's real over what's manufactured.

  • Woman-owned, single-mom-run business
  • Real oils and butters, never synthetic substitutes
  • Colored with clay, root powders, and activated charcoal — never synthetic dye
  • Hand-poured, made with care
  • Every ingredient sourced and documented
