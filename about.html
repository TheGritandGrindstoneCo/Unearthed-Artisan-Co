Scented by nature. Colored by the earth.

My father used to take us hiking every weekend — he loved being outdoors and eating well, nothing processed. That's where I first learned to trust what's real over what's manufactured, a lesson I held onto even after I lost him young and grieved more than I ever expected to. As a single mom, I raised my son the same way he raised me, and that belief never left me: honest ingredients, straight from the earth.

Every bar of soap is mixed, poured, and cured for six weeks. Every jar of lotion is blended slowly from grass-fed tallow and organic oils — the same lipids your skin already makes on its own. Nothing rushed. Nothing cut. Just done right.

For my father.

Handcrafted in the USA. Nothing to hide.
