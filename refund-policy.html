Good to Know

Refund & Return Policy

Every order is handmade and packed with care in small batches. Because soap, lotion, and lip balm are personal-care items, we handle refunds and returns a little differently than off-the-shelf retail — here's exactly what to expect.

Last updated: September 2026

Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery at unearthedartisanco@gmail.com or through our contact form, with your order details and a photo, and we'll make it right with a replacement or a full refund.

Opened or Used Products

For hygiene reasons, we're not able to accept returns or exchanges on soap, lotion, or lip balm once it's been opened or used — the same standard most handmade and cosmetic sellers follow. If you're unsure which scent or formula is right for you, the ingredient and fragrance notes on each product page are there to help, or reach out before ordering and we're glad to help you choose.

Order Changes & Cancellations

Need to change or cancel an order? Contact us as soon as possible. We can accommodate most changes or cancellations within 24 hours of ordering, before it's packed for shipment. Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be changed or cancelled, and our standard return terms above apply.

Lost or Delayed Shipments

Nationwide orders ship via USPS, and local deliveries run twice a week within our Simi Valley, CA delivery area. We can't control carrier delays, but if your tracking shows something unusual — lost, stalled, or misdelivered — let us know and we'll help track it down or file a carrier claim.

Bundles & Gift Sets

Items purchased as part of a bundle or gift set follow the same policy above, applied to the set as a whole rather than to individual pieces within it.

Refund Processing

Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method, typically within 5–10 business days depending on your bank. Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error — a wrong or damaged item.

Questions?

Reach out any time at unearthedartisanco@gmail.com or through our contact form — we'll make it right.

This policy is provided for general information and doesn't limit any rights you may have under applicable consumer protection law.
