Good to Know

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you're looking for? Fill out our quick contact form and we'll help however we can.

What ingredients do you use?

Organic plant oils, essential oils, grass-fed tallow, organic shea butter, and natural clay, root, and mineral pigments — never synthetic dye or synthetic fragrance. Every ingredient is sourced and documented. Scented by nature, colored by the earth — that's not just our tagline, it's exactly how we formulate.

What are the benefits of the different ingredients?

Every ingredient earns its place. Grass-fed tallow is naturally rich in vitamins A, D, E, and K, and closely mirrors the lipids your skin already makes, so it moisturizes deeply without feeling heavy. Organic plant oils like jojoba and rosehip seed oil add lightweight, antioxidant-rich hydration, while organic shea butter brings extra richness for drier skin. Clay and Dead Sea mud gently draw out impurities, making them well suited to oily or blemish-prone skin, and activated charcoal — a fine, non-abrasive powder that adsorbs impurities without scrubbing — offers a gentle daily facial cleanse, balanced by our moisturizing tallow base so it never feels stripping. For color, we turn to root powders and clay instead of synthetic dye — and our essential oils, like calming lavender or bright citrus, do double duty as our only fragrance. Beeswax finishes the job in our lip balms, forming a protective barrier that seals in moisture. And because we choose organic wherever nature allows it, you can trust these ingredients were grown without synthetic pesticides too — real, from the ground up.

Do you use synthetic fragrance or dye?

Never. Our soap and lotion get their scent from essential oils, while our lip balms use organic, food-safe flavor oils instead — vanilla, peppermint, and guava. Color comes from natural clay, root powders, and activated charcoal, never synthetic dye.

Are your products vegan?

No. Our soap, lotion, and lip balm are made with grass-fed tallow and beeswax — both animal-derived ingredients we use deliberately: tallow is rich in vitamins and closely mirrors the lipids your own skin already produces, and beeswax forms a natural, breathable barrier that helps lip balm lock in moisture. If you're interested in a plant-based option, please fill out our quick contact form and we would be happy to discuss what's available.

Are your products regulated?

Yes — our soap, lotion, and lip balm are formulated and labeled to meet FDA cosmetic labeling requirements. We don't make drug or medical claims; these are cosmetic products meant for everyday care.

Will these work for sensitive skin?

Several of our formulas — like Quiet Clay soap — are fragrance-free and made with sensitive skin in mind. As with any new skincare product, we recommend a small patch test first.

How long does soap take to cure?

Every bar is mixed and poured, then cured for six weeks before it's ready to ship. That resting period lets the saponification process fully complete and excess water evaporate, resulting in a harder, longer-lasting bar that's gentle and ready for everyday use.

How long does a bar of soap last?

How long a bar lasts really depends on how it's used — a single person showering daily might get 3–5 weeks from one bar, while a bar shared by a household, or used more than once a day, will go faster. Keeping it on a draining soap dish between uses (rather than letting it sit in water) helps any bar last longer.

Can I mix and match scents?

Absolutely — mix and match soap scents freely, or check out our Bundles & Gift Sets on the shop page, where you can save up to 15% depending on which scents you pick.
