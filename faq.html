Good to Know

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you're looking for? Email us at unearthedartisanco@gmail.com and we'll help however we can.

What ingredients do you use?

Organic plant oils, grass-fed tallow, shea butter, and natural clay, root, and mineral pigments — never synthetic dye or synthetic fragrance. Every ingredient is sourced and documented.

Are your products vegan?

No. Our soap, lotion, and lip balm are made with grass-fed tallow, an animal-derived ingredient we use because it's close to the lipids your own skin already makes. If you're looking for a plant-based option, reach out and we can talk about what's available.

What are the benefits of the different ingredients?

Grass-fed tallow closely mirrors the lipids your skin already makes, so it moisturizes deeply without feeling heavy. Organic plant oils like jojoba and rosehip seed oil add lightweight, antioxidant-rich moisture, while shea butter brings extra richness for dry skin. Clay and Dead Sea mud gently draw out impurities, which is why they're well suited to oily or blemish-prone skin, and activated charcoal offers a gentle daily facial cleanse. Root powders and clay give every bar its natural color instead of synthetic dye, and beeswax helps lip balm form a protective, moisture-sealing barrier.

Do you use synthetic fragrance or dye?

Never. Scent comes from organic essential and flavor oils, and color comes from natural clay, root powders, and activated charcoal.

Will these work for sensitive skin?

Several of our formulas — like Quiet Clay soap — are fragrance-free and made with sensitive skin in mind. As with any new skincare product, we recommend a small patch test first.

How long does soap take to cure?

Every bar is mixed, poured, and cured for six weeks before it's ready to ship — that's what gives cold-process soap its long-lasting, gentle bar.

How long does a bar of soap last?

With regular daily use, a bar typically lasts about 4–6 weeks. Keeping it on a draining soap dish (rather than sitting in water) between uses helps it last longer.

Where are you located, and can I pick up my order locally?

We're a home-based studio in Simi Valley, CA (93065). Local pickup is free — once you order, we'll reach out to arrange a pickup time.

Do you ship nationwide?

Yes — every order ships from within the USA via USPS. See our Bag & Shipping page for full rates and an order estimate.

How do I actually place an order?

Add items to your bag and hit "Checkout" — you'll be taken to a secure Stripe payment page to enter your card details. Once payment goes through, we'll follow up to confirm delivery details.

Can I mix and match scents?

Absolutely — mix and match soap scents freely, or check out our Bundles & Gift Sets on the shop page for a flat price no matter which scents you pick.

What if something's wrong with my order?

Email us at unearthedartisanco@gmail.com and we'll make it right.

Are your products regulated or tested?

Yes — our soap, lotion, and lip balm are formulated and labeled to meet FDA cosmetic labeling requirements. We don't make drug or medical claims; these are cosmetic products meant for everyday care.
