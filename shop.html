Teak Soap Dish

Solid teak · Made in the USA · 4.2" × 3"

A low-profile dish that keeps your bar high and dry, so it lasts longer.

Details

Deep zig-zag channels let water run straight off your soap, so it dries between uses. Made from teak — the durable, rot-resistant wood boat builders trust — and carved from one solid piece with no glue, nails, or slats, so it will never come apart. A water-resistant finish and non-slip pads keep it steady and out of standing water. Pair with a Sisal Soap Saver Bag: the dish keeps your bar dry, and the bag uses up every last sliver.

Each$9.95

Sisal Soap Saver Bag

Natural sisal · Made in the USA · 6" × 3.5"

A washcloth, soap saver, and scrubber in one.

Details

Woven from natural sisal — the fiber of the agave plant — this little drawstring bag gives the last slivers of your soap a second life. Drop in your soap ends, wet, and lather: the scraps foam right up as you wash, with a gently exfoliating scrub. Wring it out and hang it by the drawstring to dry between uses. Plant-based, plastic-free, and compostable at the end of its life.

Each$5.99
